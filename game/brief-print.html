HOLLOW — MISSION BRIEF

STUDENT READ-AHEAD · ROLES · HOW YOU WIN ONE PAGE · SHARE BEFORE MAPS

COMMANDER’S INTENT

Infiltrate the blackout node, recover three access keys, open the console-room door (D3), jack into the core, and extract on the chopper when it is on station. Nobody has the whole picture — same floor, different sheets. Talk. Minimize emissions. If you get lost, read a wall code out loud. Success is the clone on the drive and the Operator on the pad.

HOW YOU WIN

  • Clone the core onto the drive, then extract.
  • Rescue: POW follows the Operator. Both stand on the yellow X while the chopper is on station.
  • Virus: plant at the console, then the Operator stands on the yellow X while it is on station.
  • Walk onto the pad. Do not board until the chopper is on station.

HOW YOU FAIL

  • Caught — security reaches the Operator.
  • Left behind — chopper leaves and you are not on the pad.
  • Blackout ends — power returns at 10:00.
  • Rescue without the POW on the pad does not count. Virus without the plant does not count.

ROLES — DO NOT TRADE SHEETS

OPERATOR

Headset. You are the only person in the site. You have LiDAR — no paper map.

Scan quiet (trigger). Interact / download / POW follow: X. Hold X to download, then to plant virus. Stay close to the POW or they slow down.

WATCH

Full floor + wall codes. You keep them alive in the halls.

You have: tripwires, safe spaces, patrol posts, shutoff boxes N/C/T (no digits). You do not have keys, doors, core, or LZ.

ACCESS

Full floor + wall codes. You get them to the core and home.

You have: keys 1–3, blast doors, core G, LZ X, infil P, beacon refills. You do not have tripwires. 3-person: you also have shutoff codes.

CONTROLLER · 4-PERSON ONLY

No map. Run the clock and the radio. Do not take a navigator sheet.

You have: 10:00 clock, objective ticks, both legends, shutoff codes N/C/T. POW location comes from the Operator after they choose rescue.

MISSION ORDER

1 · KEYS

Access calls amber keys. Recover all three. Optional 1-key doors are shortcuts — D3 is not optional.

2 · CONSOLE

Open D3 (needs all 3 keys). Jack in at G: press X, then hold X until the download finishes.

3 · FORK

Choose Rescue POW or Plant the Virus. Rescue: Operator reads the wall code, then I UNDERSTAND starts the clock. Press X on the POW. Stay close.

4 · EXTRACT

Chopper inbound, then on station. Access calls the yellow X. Watch keeps them off tripwires the whole way.

RADIO

  • Operator lost? They read a wall code. Both Watch and Access can place it.
  • Before a hall, Watch answers: “wired?” Access answers: “keys / door / LZ?”
  • Safe space (Watch) — security will not kill them there. Break, reorient, leave quiet.
  • Shutoff: Watch finds the wall box and calls N, C, or T. Whoever has the codes reads the digits. All tripwires off for 15 seconds. You can use a box more than once.

WHAT THE OPERATOR SEES

  • Amber — access keys
  • Yellow beams — tripwires (Watch)
  • Green rooms — safe space (Watch)
  • Red — security
  • Bright green person — POW
  • Yellow pad — LZ. Walk on it when the chopper is on station
